## Aim

To design and execute prompts using different prompt engineering techniques and evaluate the generated outputs using a real-world use case from Unit 5, namely the Smart Agriculture System.

## Explanation

Prompt engineering is the process of designing and structuring instructions given to Artificial Intelligence (AI) models to generate meaningful, accurate, and relevant responses. A well-designed prompt helps an AI system understand the user's requirements and produce the expected output.

Different prompting techniques are used to control the response format, improve accuracy, provide context, and complete missing information.

In this experiment, four different prompting techniques are implemented and evaluated using the Smart Agriculture System.

### Types of Prompt Engineering

1. **Straightforward Prompting:** Giving a direct instruction to the AI model.
2. **Tabular Format Prompting:** Asking the AI model to organize information in rows and columns.
3. **Preceding Question Prompting:** Providing related questions before the main instruction to establish context.
4. **Missing Word Prompting:** Providing incomplete sentences with blanks for the AI model to complete.

## Use Case – Smart Agriculture System (Unit 5)

Smart agriculture is a modern farming approach that uses advanced technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Internet of Things (IoT), drones, GPS, and automated irrigation systems to improve agricultural productivity.

Traditional farming methods often face challenges such as unpredictable weather conditions, water scarcity, crop diseases, labour shortages, and inefficient resource utilization.

Smart agriculture provides solutions to these problems by collecting real-time agricultural data and using intelligent systems to analyze it. IoT sensors monitor soil moisture, temperature, and humidity, while AI algorithms analyze the collected information to support better farming decisions.

### Objectives of Smart Agriculture

- Increase crop production.
- Reduce operational costs.
- Conserve water and other natural resources.
- Detect crop diseases at an early stage.
- Promote sustainable farming practices.


**Major Components:**

- **IoT Sensors:** Collect information about soil moisture, temperature, and humidity.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** Analyzes agricultural data and predicts crop diseases.
- **Drones:** Monitor large agricultural fields and identify crop-related problems.
- **GPS Technology:** Supports accurate field mapping and precision farming.
- **Smart Irrigation:** Automatically controls water supply according to soil conditions.

## Procedure

### 1. Straightforward Prompting

**Definition:**

Straightforward prompting is a basic prompt engineering technique in which a direct and specific instruction is given to an AI model without providing unnecessary background information. It is useful for obtaining quick and simple answers.

**Prompt:**

"Explain how Artificial Intelligence is used in smart agriculture. Mention five applications and three major benefits in simple terms."

**Generated Output:**

Artificial Intelligence plays an important role in smart agriculture by helping farmers improve farming operations through automation, prediction, and data analysis.

**Applications of AI in Smart Agriculture:**

1. **Crop Monitoring:** AI analyzes crop images to identify unhealthy plants.
2. **Disease Detection:** AI identifies crop diseases at an early stage.
3. **Weather Prediction:** AI analyzes weather information to help farmers plan agricultural activities.
4. **Soil Analysis:** AI evaluates soil conditions to support better crop management.
5. **Automated Farming:** AI supports the operation of intelligent agricultural machines.

**Major Benefits:**

- **Increased Productivity:** Helps farmers improve crop production.
- **Reduced Costs:** Minimizes unnecessary use of fertilizers, pesticides, and labour.
- **Efficient Resource Management:** Helps conserve water and other agricultural resources.

**Evaluation:**

The straightforward prompt generated a clear and relevant response. It provided the required applications and benefits without unnecessary information.

### 2. Tabular Format Prompting

**Definition:**

Tabular format prompting is a technique in which the AI model is instructed to organize information in a structured table containing rows and columns. It makes information easier to understand, compare, and analyze.

**Prompt:**

"Prepare a detailed table showing five important technologies used in smart agriculture, their functions, applications, and advantages."

**Generated Output:**

| Technology | Function | Application | Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| IoT Sensors | Collect environmental data | Soil moisture monitoring | Saves water |
| Artificial Intelligence | Analyze agricultural data | Disease prediction | Improves crop health |
| Drones | Capture aerial images | Field monitoring | Saves time |
| GPS | Identify accurate locations | Precision farming | Improves field management |
| Smart Irrigation | Automate water supply | Crop watering | Reduces water wastage |

**Evaluation:**

The tabular format prompt successfully organized information into a structured format. It made the comparison of agricultural technologies easier and improved the readability of the generated output.

### 3. Preceding Question Prompting

**Definition:**

Preceding question prompting is a technique in which one or more related questions are presented before the main instruction. These questions provide context and guide the AI model to generate a more meaningful and detailed response.

**Prompt:**

"Why do farmers face difficulties in traditional farming? What are the major problems caused by water scarcity, crop diseases, and unpredictable weather? How can Artificial Intelligence and IoT technologies solve these problems? Explain how smart agriculture improves farming productivity and sustainability."

**Generated Output:**

Traditional farming faces several challenges, including unpredictable weather, water scarcity, pest attacks, crop diseases, and increasing labour costs.

Smart agriculture helps overcome these challenges using modern technologies.

1. **Water Scarcity:** IoT-based soil moisture sensors monitor the water content in the soil and help automate irrigation.
2. **Crop Diseases:** AI-powered image processing systems identify diseases by analyzing photographs of crops.
3. **Unpredictable Weather:** AI-based forecasting systems analyze weather information and help farmers plan agricultural activities.
4. **Labour Shortage:** Automated machines and smart equipment reduce the need for manual labour.
5. **Low Productivity:** Data analysis helps farmers select suitable crops and improve agricultural management.

By integrating AI and IoT, smart agriculture improves productivity, reduces resource wastage, and supports sustainable farming.

**Evaluation:**

The preceding question prompt generated a detailed and context-based explanation. The initial questions helped the AI model understand the agricultural problems before explaining the technological solutions.

### 4. Missing Word Prompting

**Definition:**

Missing word prompting is a technique in which incomplete sentences or statements containing blank spaces are provided to an AI model. The model identifies and fills the missing words based on the given context.

**Prompt:**

"Complete the following sentences related to smart agriculture by filling in the appropriate missing words.

1. IoT sensors are used to monitor soil ______.
2. AI stands for ______.
3. Drones are used for aerial monitoring of ______.
4. Automated irrigation systems help to conserve ______.
5. GPS technology is used for accurate farm ______.
6. AI helps farmers detect crop ______ at an early stage.
7. Smart agriculture improves agricultural ______."

**Generated Output:**

1. Moisture
2. Artificial Intelligence
3. Crops
4. Water
5. Mapping
6. Diseases
7. Productivity

**Evaluation:**

The missing word prompt successfully completed the incomplete sentences with relevant agricultural terms. It demonstrated the ability of an AI model to understand context and identify appropriate words.
